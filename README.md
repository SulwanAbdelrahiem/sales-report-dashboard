# Sales Report Dashboard

**Tool:** Power BI (DAX)

## Overview
A Power BI dashboard analyzing Q1 2019 point-of-sale data across three cities and product lines, with a day-level date slicer for drill-down into daily sales trends.

## Key Metrics
- Total COGS: **307.59K**
- Total sales: **322.97K** | Total gross income: **15.38K**
- Sales split across 3 cities (Naypyitaw, Yangon, Mandalay) and 3 branches (A, B, C), each close to an even 33% share
- Sales by gender: Female 168K vs. Male 155K
- Top product line by income: Food and Beverages, followed by Sports & Travel and Electronic Accessories

## Key Insight
A balanced sales distribution across cities and branches points to a stable market, with Food & Beverages slightly leading as the top-earning product line.

## Tools & Techniques
- DAX measures for daily sales and gross income trends
- Breakdowns by city, branch, gender, and product line
